# Investment format

Investment exists to make the commercial decision legible.

## Required information order
For each option or recommended investment, present:
1. **Included scope** — offer/workstream names and units where relevant.
2. **Commercial assumptions** — period, volume, capacity, minimums, variables, taxes if configured, and unresolved inputs.
3. **Why this design** — short rationale tied to the client's situation.
4. **Investment** — fixed, recurring, usage, milestone, or variable components with payment terms.
5. **Consolidated total** — only when calculable; otherwise show a range, formula, or unresolved variable.

## Option policy
Use one recommendation when the commercial answer is singular. Use A/B/C, Good/Better/Best, or named scenarios only when each option materially changes scope, service level, capacity, coverage, cadence, duration, implementation risk, access to expertise, or expected business utility. Do not create fake differentiation.

## Pricing integrity
Preserve configured units; do not apply unauthorized discounts; do not add taxes/fees unless configured; do not total unknown variables; label estimates and assumptions; optional future services must not enter contracted totals.
