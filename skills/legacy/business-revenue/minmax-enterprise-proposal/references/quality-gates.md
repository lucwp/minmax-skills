# Quality gates

A proposal is ready only when all material gates pass.

## Commercial
Recommendation is explicit; scope maps to configured offers or clearly identified custom work; contracted and optional work are unambiguous; pricing units/totals reconcile; discounts comply; next step is concrete.

## Evidence
No unsupported guarantees; no invented metrics/logos/testimonials/client facts; estimates and assumptions are labelled; case-study proof is not generalized beyond evidence.

## Narrative
Executive summary passes the decision memo test; What-we-understood is client-specific; recommendation explains tradeoffs; pages do not repeat the same idea in multiple containers; filler/internal jargon is removed.

## Visual
No clipping/overflow; no microscopic type workaround; brand tokens are consistent; logos preserve aspect ratio and contrast; commercial totals are legible.

## Technical
Profile validates; proposal JSON validates; HTML preflight passes; required files are present and paths resolve when used.
