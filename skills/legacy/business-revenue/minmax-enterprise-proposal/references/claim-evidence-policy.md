# Claim and evidence policy

## Evidence classes

Treat claims as one of:

- **Verified provider fact** — supported by an authoritative company source.
- **Verified client fact** — supplied by the client/user or supported by a client source.
- **Approved proof** — metric, case study, testimonial, or logo with known permission status.
- **Third-party evidence** — externally sourced research with a traceable source.
- **Internal estimate** — modelled or directional; label as estimate.
- **Assumption** — unverified; state it explicitly or keep it out of client-facing copy.

## Rules

- Never upgrade an assumption to a fact because it sounds plausible.
- Never turn a historical case-study result into a guaranteed future outcome.
- Never claim causality from correlational evidence.
- Never use customer logos or testimonials as endorsements when permission is unknown.
- Prefer proof that addresses the buyer's actual risk: credibility, feasibility, adoption, economics, delivery, or governance.
- Use fewer stronger proof points rather than a logo wall with no decision value.
