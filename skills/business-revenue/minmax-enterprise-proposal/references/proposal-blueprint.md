# Proposal blueprint

The structure is adaptive. Use only the pages required to support the buying decision.

## 1. Cover
Provider + client, proposal title, short context line, issue date, validity, optional logos/imagery.

## 2. Executive summary
State the decision, why it matters now, business consequence, recommended scope, investment logic, and next step. Avoid a meeting recap.

## 3. What we understood
Show specific understanding of the client's current state, tension/opportunity, constraints, and decision criteria. Include at least one concrete business consequence.

## 4. Recommendation and rationale
Explain what is recommended, why this shape fits, what tradeoff it makes, and what is intentionally excluded. If the work has a real topology, show it as sequence, parallel workstreams, hierarchy, or transformation.

## 5. Scope
Use one or more scope pages. Connect business value to operating detail and tangible delivery. Avoid dumping every internal task.

## 6. Delivery model — conditional
Use only when governance, roles, cadence, implementation, or dependencies are central to purchase confidence.

## 7. Proof — conditional
Use only when verified proof reduces a relevant buyer risk. Place proof near the claim it supports when possible.

## 8. Investment
Follow `investment-format.md`. Separate scope, assumptions, rationale, components, and consolidated total.

## 9. Conditions for success
State client dependencies, decision rights, access, inputs, approvals, participation, data, or implementation conditions that materially affect delivery. Avoid generic legal boilerplate.

## 10. Evolution — conditional
Show credible next-stage options without making them look contracted. Keep this consultative, not catalog-like.

## 11. Next steps
Two short paragraphs plus one explicit decision/action. Describe what happens after acceptance without recreating a project plan.

## Density guidance
Narrative page: 2–5 short paragraphs or 1–3 paragraphs plus a structured callout. Scope page: 1 value paragraph + 3–5 how-it-works bullets + 3–5 deliverables. Investment page: keep commercial facts visible without microscopic type. If content does not fit, split the page.
