# Semantic page manifest

Use this planning model for complex proposals before rendering.

Each page should define:

- `id` — stable page id;
- `section` — commercial section name;
- `purpose` — what decision or understanding the page should enable;
- `relationship` — `narrative`, `sequence`, `parallel`, `hierarchy`, `comparison`, `transformation`, or `commercial`;
- `density` — `light`, `standard`, or `dense`;
- `emphasis` — `low`, `medium`, or `high`;
- `family` — `cover`, `executive`, `narrative`, `decision`, `scope`, `sequence`, `comparison`, `commercial`, `proof`, or `next-step`;
- `split_policy` — `never`, `allowed`, or `preferred`;
- `content_source` — proposal data key(s) supplying the page.

Choose relationship from the actual operating/commercial logic. Three workstreams are not automatically three phases.
