# Consulting engagement lifecycle

Lifecycle inspiration: A-004, with state/assurance concepts from A-001, A-006 and A-007.

## 0. Qualification / proposal

Define the decision, scope, stakeholder, workstreams, evidence access, timeline, deliverables, exclusions, fees/effort, client responsibilities and success criteria.

Do not promise quantified outcomes that cannot yet be substantiated.

## 1. Setup

Create:
- engagement contract;
- stakeholder/decision map;
- workplan;
- source/data request;
- risk and assumption register;
- governance cadence;
- artifact/version structure.

## 2. Diagnose

Build the fact base and logic map. Identify the few branches that can change the answer. Verify data quality before analysis.

## 3. Analyze

Run hypothesis-linked workstreams. Persist methods, assumptions, findings and unresolved questions. Reconcile conflicting evidence.

## 4. Synthesize

Move from finding -> implication -> decision. Pressure-test against alternatives and disconfirming evidence.

## 5. Decide

Make recommendation, economics, assumptions, risks, kill criteria and conditions explicit. Run the Challenge Engine only when the user activated it; always run applicable deterministic release checks before an unconditional recommendation.

## 6. Mobilize

Translate recommendation into initiatives with owner, milestone, dependency, resources, governance and adoption plan.

## 7. Implement

Track deliverables and outcome metrics separately. Escalate assumption failures and value leakage, not just schedule slippage.

## 8. Realize benefits (F-017)

Use PMI's benefits-realization logic:
- identify intended benefits and measures;
- execute benefits management during delivery;
- sustain and verify benefits after delivery.

Maintain baseline, target, actual, variance, explanation and corrective action.

## 9. Closeout

Hand over decision record, models, data lineage, open risks, operating cadence, benefits ownership and lessons learned. Preserve what future teams need to reproduce or challenge the recommendation.
