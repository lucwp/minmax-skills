## Source hierarchy

Use these sources as framework authorities, not as evidence that a client has a specific problem. For client claims, prefer:

1. client primary systems and audited/controlled records;
2. regulator/government/official statistics;
3. company filings and attributable primary disclosures;
4. transparent original studies;
5. credible institutional/industry synthesis;
6. reputable journalism for events/context;
7. trade press or vendor claims only with corroboration when material.

## Currency rule

Before relying on a framework source for a time-sensitive methodological claim, re-check the source. A classic framework can remain useful while the surrounding implementation guidance changes. Record the access date in the engagement source ledger.
