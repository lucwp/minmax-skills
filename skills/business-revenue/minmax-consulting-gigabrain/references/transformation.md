# Transformation, cost and change

## Fit for Growth (F-012)

Use for cost transformation when the strategic question is where to cut and where to reinvest.

Sequence:
1. Identify the few differentiated capabilities that support the strategy.
2. Classify spend/capabilities into differentiating, necessary/competitive, and non-differentiating/legacy.
3. Protect or increase investment in capabilities that create advantage.
4. Remove, simplify, automate or redesign legacy/commodity cost.
5. Reinvest a defined portion of savings into growth/advantage.
6. Redesign the operating model so savings persist.

Anti-pattern: uniform percentage cuts across functions regardless of strategic role.

## Kotter 8 Steps (F-015)

Use for enterprise mobilization. Operationalize as:
- urgency/opportunity case;
- guiding coalition;
- strategic vision and linked initiatives;
- broad volunteer/advocate network;
- barriers removed;
- near-term wins;
- acceleration and scaling;
- institutionalization of new behaviors/systems.

Do not treat this as a rigid waterfall. Use it as a completeness check for mobilization and sustainment.

## Prosci ADKAR (F-014)

Use at individual/role level to diagnose adoption barriers:
- Awareness of need;
- Desire to participate/support;
- Knowledge of how;
- Ability to perform;
- Reinforcement to sustain.

Map each affected persona/role. The lowest unresolved stage is often the binding adoption constraint.

Do not use ADKAR as a substitute for project delivery, operating-model design or executive sponsorship.

## Change evidence

Track leading and lagging indicators separately:
- participation/exposure;
- understanding;
- capability demonstration;
- behavior adoption;
- process/system usage;
- operational KPI impact;
- realized benefits.
