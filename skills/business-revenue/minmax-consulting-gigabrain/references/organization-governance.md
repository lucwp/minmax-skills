# Organization, operating model and governance

## 7-S diagnostic (F-002)

Use to test organizational alignment across strategy, structure, systems, style, staff, skills and shared values.

For each element capture:
- current state;
- evidence;
- target state implied by strategy;
- gap;
- interdependency with other elements;
- performance consequence.

The framework is systemic: a structural change alone is rarely sufficient when skills, incentives, systems or leadership behavior remain misaligned.

## Modern operating model: Organize to Value (F-003)

Use for current operating-model redesign. Start from the strategy-to-performance gap and design an integrated system rather than an org chart.

Translate the public framework into these consulting questions:
- What value outcomes and purpose anchor the design?
- Which capabilities and processes are differentiating?
- What talent, behaviors and leadership model are required?
- What rewards reinforce those behaviors?
- What footprint/sourcing choices are needed?
- What technology and management systems enable the model?
- Where are decision rights and accountability located?

## Deloitte strategy-to-operating-model chain (F-013)

Use as a complementary implementation lens:

strategy -> business model -> required capabilities -> operating model -> people/process/technology/management systems.

A redesign should show traceability from strategy to each major operating-model choice.

## RAPID decision rights (F-011)

Use when decisions are slow, duplicated, politicized or ownerless.

Roles:
- Recommend
- Agree
- Perform
- Input
- Decide

Rules:
- One clear `Decide` owner for a major decision whenever feasible.
- Do not confuse consultation (`Input`) with veto (`Agree`).
- Map recurring decision classes, not every trivial decision.
- Pair decision rights with information, cadence and escalation rules.

## Governance output

For a transformation or operating model include:
- decision-rights map;
- forums/cadence;
- KPI and risk review;
- escalation thresholds;
- owner for benefits;
- owner for assumption revalidation;
- exceptions process.
