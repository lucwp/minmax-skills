# Adaptive inquiry question bank

Use this as a bank, not a questionnaire. Ask only questions that remain unresolved after reviewing existing sources.

## Wave A — business and proposal issuer
- What name should appear as the provider on proposals? Is there a different legal name?
- What does the business sell in one sentence, without marketing adjectives?
- Which markets/languages/currencies should the proposal system support by default?
- Who normally signs or issues proposals, and what title/contact should appear?
- Are there multiple brands or business units that need separate profiles?

## Wave B — offer architecture
For each proposal-relevant offer ask about official name/descriptor, fit, business value, delivery, deliverables, exclusions, client dependencies, pricing, discount authority, and supporting proof.

## Wave C — positioning and buying context
Ask about best-fit and poor-fit customers, champion/approver/user/procurement roles, trigger events, alternatives, demonstrable differentiators, buyer outcomes/risks, recurring objections, actual decision criteria, and words/promises to avoid.

## Wave D — commercial rules
Ask about payment terms, validity, taxes, minimums, setup/travel/pass-through costs, capacity limits, scope changes, negotiability, scenario policy, and mandatory procurement/legal clauses.

## Wave E — proof and evidence
Ask which metrics, outcomes, logos, testimonials, and third-party sources are verified/approved; which claims are forbidden; and whether guarantees, SLAs, credits, or performance commitments are formally authorized.

## Wave F — brand and visual system
Ask about logo variants, colors, font family names and fallbacks, 3–5 desired visual adjectives, anti-adjectives, imagery policy, density, shape language, footer/confidentiality language, client-logo behavior, and reference designs.

## Wave G — proposal workflow
Ask about default language, page format, thesis-first behavior, mandatory/optional/forbidden sections, About-us behavior, next-step CTA, email generation, and required output formats.
