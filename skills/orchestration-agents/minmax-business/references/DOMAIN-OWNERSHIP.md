# Domain Ownership Map

Use this reference only when routing or authority is ambiguous. Route by the decision owner, not by keywords.

## Strategy

Own strategic diagnosis, where-to-play/how-to-win choices, business or corporate strategy, growth strategy, market entry, portfolio choices, strategic priorities, resource allocation, scenarios, transformation strategy, and strategic roadmaps.

Does not own specialist execution inside Product, Marketing, Sales, Commercial, Customer, or Operations.

## Market Intelligence

Own market research, customer research, ICP evidence, segmentation evidence, buyer research, JTBD research, competitive intelligence, category research, market sizing, trend research, market signals, and research-backed claims.

Supply evidence to other domains. Do not convert evidence ownership into downstream decision authority.

## Product

Own product vision, product strategy, discovery, opportunity identification, PMF, MVP design, assumption testing, product experiments, roadmap, prioritization, feature decisions, and product portfolio decisions.

Consume Market Intelligence when relevant. Do not own campaign or sales execution.

## Marketing

Own marketing strategy, messaging, narrative, brand, content, SEO/GEO/AEO, social, email marketing, lifecycle marketing, paid media, demand generation, campaigns, launches, events, referrals, CRO, marketing experiments, and marketing measurement.

Market Intelligence owns underlying market evidence. Commercial owns pricing authority. Sales owns opportunity progression.

## Sales

Own prospecting execution, account research for selling, outbound, meeting preparation, discovery, qualification, stakeholder mapping, multithreading, deal strategy, demos, objections, follow-up, mutual action plans, opportunity progression, call analysis, sales coaching, and pre-close handoff.

May recommend Commercial actions but cannot override Commercial policy.

## Commercial

Own pricing, packaging economics, willingness-to-pay decisions, discount governance, deal desk, commercial policy, business cases, ROI economics, commercial terms, negotiation economics, approval routing, channel economics, commercial forecasting, and RFP commercial decisions.

Decide which economic and contractual conditions are acceptable. Do not own prospecting or campaign execution.

## Partnerships

Own partnership strategy, ecosystem strategy, partner identification and evaluation, strategic alliances, technology partnerships, channel partnerships, referral partnerships, co-selling, co-marketing partnerships, partner programs, partner tiers, partner enablement, joint business plans, and partner performance.

Use Commercial for economics, pricing, terms, and approval policy. Use RevOps for partner-pipeline systems and revenue infrastructure.

## Customer

Own onboarding, customer success, account management, success planning, customer experience, Voice of Customer, adoption, post-sale stakeholder management, health and risk, retention, expansion, renewal, QBRs, and service experience.

Use RevOps for system-level lifecycle infrastructure and measurement.

## Revenue Operations

Own revenue architecture, funnel architecture, lifecycle stages, CRM, pipeline architecture, stage governance, lead routing, territories, capacity, quota mechanics, forecast governance, pipeline coverage, attribution, SLAs, RevTech, automation, revenue data quality, revenue analytics, conversion, velocity, CAC/LTV, sales efficiency, marketing efficiency, and forecast accuracy.

Govern the revenue system. Do not assume authority over every commercial, sales, marketing, or customer decision executed inside that system.

## Business Operations

Own operating model, process design, process mapping, SOPs, RACI, governance, organizational interfaces, decision rights, operating cadence, cross-functional workflows, capacity planning, bottleneck analysis, cycle time, internal transformation, and project operating models.

RevOps is scoped to the revenue engine. BizOps may span the company.

## Management

When a dedicated management skill exists, route people-management work such as delegation, one-on-ones, coaching, team design, performance management, managerial leverage, meeting systems, and motivation there rather than forcing it into BizOps.

## Cross-domain default precedence

Use this default dependency order only when the request truly spans these stages:

Market Intelligence → Strategy → Product/Offer → Commercial → Marketing/Sales/Partnerships → Revenue Operations → Customer → Business Operations.

Skip irrelevant stages.
